Data processing agreement

This agreement is between Eltio Studio Ltd (company number 15127751, registered in England and Wales), "Eltio", and the company or person using Eltio Systems, "you". It forms part of the terms and meets Article 28 of the UK GDPR. If it conflicts with the terms, this agreement wins on data.

1. Roles

  • You are the controller of the personal data you put into Systems: your crew, clients, contacts and their details.
  • Eltio is your processor. We process that data only to run Systems for you, and only on your documented instructions, which are these terms and how you use the app.
  • If we're ever required by law to do something else with it, we'll tell you first unless the law stops us.

2. What we process

  • Purpose: showing your dashboard, timesheets, invoices, crew paperwork and drafts.
  • People: you, your team, crew, freelancers, clients, agencies and suppliers.
  • Data: names, email addresses, phone numbers, job and booking details, rates, hours, invoice and payment details, and anything else you choose to add. Crew paperwork can include bank details, UTR and National Insurance numbers and right-to-work status.
  • Email: if you forward email to your Systems address, the subject, sender, date and a short preview of each work email. Attachments and full message bodies aren't kept.
  • Duration: for as long as you use Systems, then as section 7 says.

3. Where your data lives

  • Your records are kept in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. Google holds it under your agreement with Google, not ours. The app can only open files it made or files you open with it.
  • Forwarded email waits on Eltio's mail service until your app collects it, which is usually minutes. It's deleted as soon as it's collected, and after 30 days at most if it never is.
  • Bank statements you upload are read on your own device and aren't sent to us.

4. Our duties

  • Everyone at Eltio who can reach your data is bound to keep it confidential.
  • We keep appropriate security in place: encrypted connections everywhere, access limited to the files the app made, private forwarding addresses protected by a secret key, short storage times, and no copies kept in our own systems.
  • We help you answer requests from people exercising their data rights, and with security, breach and impact assessments where our part is relevant.
  • We tell you without undue delay, and within 48 hours, after we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data.
  • We give you the information you need to show we meet this agreement, and allow reasonable audits with 30 days' notice, at your cost, no more than once a year unless there's been a breach.

5. Sub-processors

You agree we can use these. We'll give you 30 days' notice before adding or replacing one, and you can object or stop using Systems.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. runs the private forwarding addresses and holds forwarded work email until your app collects it.
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the app's code and website. No customer data is stored there.

Google provides your Drive and sign-in under your own agreement with Google, so it isn't our sub-processor. We hold each sub-processor to data terms at least as protective as these.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Where a sub-processor handles data outside the UK, the transfer is covered by UK adequacy regulations or by the ICO's International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. When you stop

  • Your sheet stays yours in your Drive: keep it, export it or delete it.
  • Deleting your Systems address in Help removes anything waiting there straight away. Otherwise it's gone within 30 days.
  • We confirm deletion in writing if you ask.

8. Liability and law

Liability under this agreement follows the terms. This agreement is under the law of England and Wales. Questions: hello@eltio.studio. ICO registration ZC277031.
