Privacy

Updated 19 October 2026

Aglo is made by Eltio Studio Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. The short version: Aglo doesn't collect your data. Everything it needs stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

  • The apps you guard. Apple's Screen Time gives Aglo sealed tokens, not app names or usage. Aglo can't see what you use or for how long, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The camera. For press-ups and squats, the front camera counts reps on your phone. No video or images are recorded, saved or sent.
  • Touch grass photos. Checked on your phone for grass or plants, then thrown away. Not saved to your library or sent anywhere.
  • Steps. Counted from your iPhone's motion data, on the phone.
  • Wallpapers. Saved to your photos only when you ask. Aglo can add to your library but can't see it.
  • Your settings and time given back. Stored on your phone only.

What leaves your phone

  • Your city. Search for it, or let Aglo use your location once. Apple's location service turns that into a city name. Aglo doesn't track where you go.
  • Weather. Your city's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo, a weather service, to get the forecast. No account, name or device ID goes with them.
  • Purchases. If you buy Aglo Plus, it's bought and managed through Apple. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing data, no analytics tied to you.

Children

Aglo is for people managing their own phone. It isn't designed for parents controlling a child's device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: hello@eltio.studio.
