2.Out & Scout
iPhone app (in testing)
(a viewfinder for the recce)
Line up the shot, see where the sun’s going to be, and walk away with a shot list. Pick your camera and lens and the phone shows you exactly that frame. A new scene starts every 100 metres you walk, and the list goes to the crew as a PDF or a link.

3.Lampwerk
iPhone app (in design)
(lighting plans for DPs and gaffers)
Plan the lighting before you’ve even got to the location. Drop in the heads you’re renting and it tells you the light levels and the amps as you move them around. It all ends up as an A4 gaffer sheet. The only colour is a gel, Medium Yellow, because it only ever means light.
Swift, SwiftUI (planned)

5.Grip
Phone app (in build)
(an AI coach for climbers and grapplers)
Film a climb or a roll and Grip shows you what your body actually did. It tracks fifteen points and your centre of mass, then pins up to three notes on the moments that matter. Hold your tape up against a mate’s, or watch the same move from the side.
