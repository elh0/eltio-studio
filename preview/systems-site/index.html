Questions.

Does it know APA and Bectu terms?

Yes. Commercials use the APA crew terms: the three day-rate bands, early, late and night calls, after midnight at 3x, the late break, weekends. TV and film use the Pact/Bectu drama terms: 1.5x overtime kept between £35 and £70, quarter hours, after 23:00, sixth days. A flat day rate works too.

I'm PAYE on a drama. Is it any use?

Yes. Payroll pays you, so there's no invoice, but Systems keeps your own record of every day and works out what the payslip should say. If they differ, you'll know which day and by how much.

Do I need a call or a demo?

No. Open it, pick your job, and it shows you round with sample data. Setup takes a few minutes and is all done on your phone.

Does it replace FreeAgent or Xero?

Not yet. It sits beside them and does the film part they don't: holds, call times, overtime, chasing in your name. Links to Xero, FreeAgent and bank feeds are next.

What about production companies?

The app checks crew days against the terms today. The company tools (advance invoices on award, the permit clock, rights and usage, payment runs) are set up on a company's own Google and Xero accounts in the pilot.

Who sees my data?

You. It lives in a spreadsheet in your Google Drive and Systems can only open the one file it made.
