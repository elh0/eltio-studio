01 · Viewfinder

Frame it at your real lens.

Pick the lenses in your kit and the iPhone shows what each one sees, with frame lines for 2.39, 1.85, 16:9 and 9:16. Try it, then press the shutter.

02 · Sun path and light read

Know when it'll be backlit.

The real sun for Knole Park today. Drag it through the day and the light on the phone, the picture and the read all change together.

03 · Shot List

Every shot, numbered.

Each shot lands in the list with its still, caption, lens, time and light. Shots you take with the shutter here land in it too.

04 · Export

Send the day as a PDF.

A plan for the day in the order the light comes round, a page per scene with its map and the sun, then a card per shot.

05 · Exposure

Exposure, without the clutter.

Tap to focus and a small sun sits beside the square. Pull it down to darken, up to brighten, two stops either way. Hold to lock focus and exposure. No panel over the picture, no numbers, no menus. Just the frame.

06 · Sky Sweep In testing

When does the sun clear the roof?

After your first shot at a new spot, if the sun crosses where you're pointing, the app offers a ten-second sweep. Follow the sun's path with the phone, it traces where the sky starts, and you get the time to be there.
