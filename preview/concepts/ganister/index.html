We keep what's standing.

Ganister is a small Sheffield practice. We retrofit gritstone terraces, mills and chapels instead of knocking them down.

See what we kept Start a project

Most of Sheffield is already built.

Knocking a building down throws away the carbon already spent quarrying its stone and sawing its timber. We start from what is there and add only what the building needs to work for another hundred years.

Ganister is the hard sandstone dug on the edges above the city for furnace linings. Most of the buildings we work on are made of its softer cousin, gritstone.

Four people, one city.

We only take work we can cycle to. It keeps us on site every week, and it means we know which quarry the stone came from before we start.
