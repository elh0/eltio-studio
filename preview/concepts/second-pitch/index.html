What's in a bar

Each bar is 55g of oats, dates and peanuts baked under the Wicker arches. It rides in a chalk bag pocket in one piece. The grade says how dark it gets, not how hard you climb.

No powders, no numbers to decode. Just food that keeps in a pocket for a week and tastes like something a friend baked.

Two climbers, one oven.

We started baking these for our own long days on the edges. Friends kept asking for them at the crag, so in 2025 we took a unit under the arches and a proper oven. We still climb most weekends, and every new bar is tested on the second pitch of something.
