Privacy

Updated 20 October 2026

Aglo is made by Eltio Studio Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. The short version: Aglo doesn't collect your data. Everything it needs stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

  • The apps you guard. Apple's Screen Time gives Aglo sealed tokens, not app names. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.
  • Time back. Your weekly figures are worked out by Apple's Screen Time report on your phone and shown only to you. Aglo can't send them anywhere, so we never see them.
  • The camera. For press-ups and squats, the front camera counts reps on your phone. No video or images are recorded, saved or sent.
  • Touch grass photos. Checked on your phone for grass or plants, then thrown away. Not saved to your library or sent anywhere.
  • Steps. Counted from your iPhone's motion data, on the phone.
  • Your settings. Stored on your phone only.
  • Wallpapers. Saved to your Photos only when you tap save.

What leaves your phone

  • Weather. Your city is sent as coordinates rounded to about 1 km to Apple WeatherKit to get the forecast. It isn't linked to you. If you let Aglo find your city, your location is used once on the phone and rounded the same way.
  • Purchases. Aglo Plus is bought and managed through Apple. We never see your payment details.

This website

If you leave your email on a-glo.com, we keep it in a private Google Sheet that only Eltio Studio can see and send one email when Aglo is out. We never share or sell it. Ask at hello@eltio.studio and we delete it.

What we don't do

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing data, no analytics tied to you.

Children

Aglo is for people managing their own phone. It isn't designed for parents controlling a child's device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: hello@eltio.studio.
