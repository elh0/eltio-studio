AGLO: QUIET PHONE · WIDGET SETS · LONDON

Widgets that fill the screen

Drawn at real iPhone sizes. On iOS 26 the biggest widget is large, so filling the page means stacking widgets: a large plus a medium is exactly one page. Each widget paints its part of the same sky, so a stack looks like one piece of glass. iOS 27 adds the full-page widget, which Aglo switches to on its own.

Every widget is in the app; you add the ones you want from the widget gallery. Long-press a widget to pick which apps and which info it shows.
