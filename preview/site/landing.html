Out & Scout

iPhone app, location scouting, in testing

Line up the shot, see where the sun’s going to be, and walk away with a shot list. Pick your camera and lens and the phone shows you exactly that frame. A new scene starts every 100 metres you walk, and the list goes to the crew as a PDF or a link.

Scout a location on your phone. See where the sun will be, frame the shot, send the crew a shot list.

(02)

Grip

Phone app, climbing and BJJ coach, in build

Film a climb or a roll and Grip shows you what your body actually did. It tracks fifteen points and your centre of mass, then pins up to three notes on the moments that matter. Hold your tape up against a mate’s, or watch the same move from the side.

Film a climb or a roll. Grip shows you what your body actually did.

(03)

Lampwerk

iPhone app, lighting plans, in design

Plan the lighting before you’ve even got to the location. Drop in the heads you’re renting and it tells you the light levels and the amps as you move them around. It all ends up as an A4 gaffer sheet. The only colour is a gel, Medium Yellow, because it only ever means light.

Plan the lighting before you get there. Light levels and amps as you go, out as one gaffer sheet.

(04)

Aglo

iPhone app, quiet home screen, in testing

Aglo takes the apps off your home screen. What’s left is one page lit by the real sun, with only the apps you chose. Everything else is still there if you type it, and the ones that pull you in ask for something first: three slow breaths, or ten press-ups.

A quiet home screen for iPhone. One page lit by the real sun, only the apps you chose, and the rest ask for something first.

Systems

Back office, production companies, in design

The admin round a shoot, run for you on the tools you already use. Late invoices get chased, holds and pencils stay in one place, receipts get matched to the job, and one short brief lands every morning. Nothing is sent without a person.

The admin round a shoot, run on the tools you already use. Invoices chased, holds kept, one brief every morning.

Spoolends

Website, film texture packs, in design

Real film texture for your edits: grain, leaks, burns and grades, all shot on real stock. The shop stays black and white so each pack’s colour comes from its own footage. Tap + to add one.

Film texture for your edits, all shot on real stock. Grain, leaks, burns and grades.
