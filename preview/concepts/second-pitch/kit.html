The brand kit.

One idea runs everything: a route line with a ring at each belay. It is the mark, the wrapper, the buttons and the bullet points.

Mark and name

second pitch

Two rings, one kink: a two-pitch route drawn the way a guidebook draws it. Always lowercase, always League Spartan Bold. The mark sits alone on the wrapper crimp and in the corner of photos; it never goes beside the name.

The name comes from the point in a long day when you stop and eat: the belay before the second pitch.
