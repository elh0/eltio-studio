A quiet phone that follows the sun

Your home page is one Aglo widget with only the apps you chose; everything else is off the home screen. Tap Search and type Instagram or TikTok to hit Aglo's lock. Tap the timecode to open Aglo's settings.

This is a click-through, not the app. The camera, step count, weather and Screen Time locks are simulated. In the real app the home page is an iOS 27 full-page widget that redraws about every 15 minutes, and the lock screen is Apple's Screen Time shield dressed in Aglo's sky: it can show two buttons, and "Unlock in Aglo" reaches the app through a notification tap.
