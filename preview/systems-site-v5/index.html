Questions.

Is it really that easy?

Yes. Open it, pick Producer, and it walks you round with a sample job. No setup call, no training. If something isn't obvious, that's ours to fix.

Does it work with Xero?

It sits beside Xero, FreeAgent or QuickBooks and does the film part they don't: crew days, overtime, permits, chasing in your name.

Does it read all my email?

No. It reads work email from the productions, agents and suppliers you pick, and you can switch it off any time. It drafts replies. It never sends one for you.

Do my crew need it?

No. Their invoices are read from email and checked either way. The live day works best when the unit is on it, but anyone who isn't just hears the usual way.

What if I don't want to go over?

Tap "Talk to the producer". They're told who wants a word, and the ask stays open until you've spoken. The app saves the walk round the set, not the conversation.

Can it read my bank statements?

If you want. Export a CSV from your bank, drop it in your Systems folder in Google Drive, and payments are matched to invoices. There's no bank login, and you can skip it completely.

Who sees my hours and overtime?

You, and the production you're working for on that job. Nobody else.

Is it free for crew?

Overtime and timesheets are free on every job, for good. If a producer runs the job on Systems, you use everything on it free. £4 a month adds your inbox, invoices, chasing and diary across all your jobs.

I have a diary service. Can I keep it?

Yes. Systems sits alongside it, and holds you take through them still show in your diary. Plenty of people try both for a while and keep whichever earns its money.

What about my agent?

Keep them. Give your agent a link and they manage your holds and offers in Systems, while you see everything as it lands.

Is it a diary service?

It does the diary part: availability, holds, offers and clash warnings. It's a flat £4 a month with no commission, ever. Keep your agent if you have one and give them a link to manage your holds.

Who can see my passport?

Nobody, until you choose to be found. Producers then see your role, credits and whether you're free. Bank, NI, address and dietary are only shared with a job you've accepted.

Commercials, TV or both?

Both. Each job carries its own terms: APA for commercials, Pact/Bectu for drama, or your own flat rate.

What does it cost?

Overtime and timesheets are free for crew. Crew Pro is £4 a month, producers £19, companies £59, with packs on top only if you want them. A producer's first real job is free, and trying it with sample data needs no card.
