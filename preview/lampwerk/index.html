Tag the set in AR

ARKit · LiDAR

Stand on the set and point the phone at the floor, the way Apple's Measure app works. Tag where talent stands, then the camera, then each head from a rail of lamps. Tap Done and the tags become the plan.

  1. 01

    Talent, then camera

    Aim the dot at the mark and tap +. Do the same where the camera goes.

  2. 02

    Each head

    Pick a head from the rail, tag where its stand goes, then set the head height. Undo or delete a tag as you go.

  3. 03

    Done

    The tags are laid out top-down, camera at the bottom looking up, with the same numbers. Send it from there.

How it works

04 steps
01

Tag in AR

Name the scene, no account. On set, point the phone at the floor and tag talent, the camera, then each lamp.

02

Tidy the plan

Done lays the tags out top-down. Drag the camera, talent or a lamp. Duplicate or mirror a head, or add lamps from the library.

03

Shape and read the light

A grid or snoot narrows the beam, a softbox or a frame of 216 opens it. Key to fill and amps update as you go. The figures are estimates, and the sheet says so.

04

Send it

Gaffer sheet, plan image or text list, through the share sheet. One page for the gaffer, the best boy and whoever is on the genny.
