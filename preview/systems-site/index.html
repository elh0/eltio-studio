Questions.

How does a production company start?

Open it and pick Producer. Crew day checks work in the app today. The rest of the office (advances on award, the permit clock, start packs, payment runs, rights) is set up on your company's own Google and Xero accounts in the pilot, without a call.

Can the whole office use it?

That's how the company pilot works: producers, production managers and coordinators each see the jobs they're on, and everything sits in your company's own Google Drive.

Does it work with Xero?

In the company pilot it sits beside Xero: each job set up as a tracking category, invoices drafted there, crew bills batched for the payment run. Direct links to Xero and FreeAgent from the app are next.

Do my crew need to use it?

No. Crew invoices are read from email as PDFs and checked either way. If your crew are on Systems too, their timesheets arrive already matched.

Does it know APA and Bectu terms?

Yes. Commercials use the APA crew terms: the three day-rate bands, early, late and night calls, after midnight at 3x, the late break, weekends. TV and film use the Pact/Bectu drama terms: 1.5x overtime kept between £35 and £70, quarter hours, after 23:00, sixth days. A flat day rate works too.

I'm PAYE on a drama. Is it any use?

Yes. Payroll pays you, so there's no invoice, but Systems keeps your own record of every day and works out what the payslip should say. If they differ, you'll know which day and by how much.

Do I need a call or a demo?

No. Open it, pick your job, and it shows you round with sample data. Setup takes a few minutes and is all done on your phone.

Does it replace FreeAgent or Xero?

Not yet. It sits beside them and does the film part they don't: holds, call times, overtime, chasing in your name. Links to Xero, FreeAgent and bank feeds are next.

Who sees my data?

You. It lives in a spreadsheet in your Google Drive and Systems can only open the one file it made.

Do I have to download anything?

No. It opens in your phone's browser and you can add it to your home screen like an app. Your laptop works too.

Commercials, TV or both?

Both. Each booking carries its own terms, so an APA commercial on Monday and a Pact/Bectu drama day on Thursday are each worked out their own way.

Does it help with VAT and tax?

It keeps a rolling 12-month total against the £90,000 VAT threshold and reminds you of Self Assessment, payments on account and Making Tax Digital dates. It doesn't file anything for you.

Do I need to be in Bectu?

No. The terms are there as a starting point for anyone. Your own deal always wins, and anything that differs is flagged for you to check.

What does it cost?

It's free to try now. Prices will be on this page before anyone is asked to pay anything.
