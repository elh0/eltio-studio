        What Grip can't do

        Some of these are "yet". We'd rather tell you here than in your first review.

        Angle

        See your hips from straight behind

        The camera can't see how far you are from the wall. Film side-on or at 45°.

        Scrambles

        Always tell two bodies apart

        In a tight scramble it can lose track of who's who. Tell it what you're wearing and it gets much better.

        Speed

        Catch every grip change

        Fast hand fighting falls between frames. Mark a window, "watch 0:12 to 0:18", and it looks closer.

        Injury

        Diagnose anything

        It isn't a physio. It can respect an injury you tell it about, but it can't find one.

        Intent

        Know what you were trying

        A deliberate drop-knee and a slip look alike. Say what you were working on.

        Coaching

        Replace a coach in the room

        It covers the sessions nobody watches. Your coach still sees what a camera can't.

        Questions

        Where should my phone go?

        Use the diagram above. For the board, side-on to 45°, so your hips show against the wall. For a roll, the side of the mat, about three metres back.

        Does it replace my coach?

        No. It covers the sessions your coach can't watch, and gives you something specific to bring to your next class.

        How does it know which one is me?

        Tell it before it watches: "I'm in the blue gi". Your partner's movement is used for context and never gets notes.

        Who sees my clips?

        Only you. Clips stay private unless you share a review or a cue card.

        Is it for beginners?

        It's built for people who train three or more times a week, already film themselves, and want the fix rather than motivation.

        I'm a coach. Can I use it with clients?

        Yes. Clients share clips to you, Grip drafts the notes, and you approve or edit them before they go out. Coach accounts open with the beta.

        When can I use it?

        The beta opens to small groups first. Join the list and we'll email you when your spot opens.
