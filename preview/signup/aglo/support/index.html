Help

Something not working, or an idea? Email hello@eltio.studio and a person replies.

An app I guarded opens without asking

Open Aglo, go to Settings, then Guarded apps, and check it's still listed. If Screen Time access was turned off in iPhone Settings, Aglo asks for it again next time you open it.

How do I open a guarded app?

Tap Unlock in Aglo on the block screen, do the task you set (breathe, press-ups, squats, a walk or a photo of grass), and the app opens for 15 minutes.

Does the camera record me?

No. Reps are counted on your phone and nothing is saved or sent.

What is time given back?

Each time Aglo stops you and you choose not to open the app, it adds 12 minutes.

I'm locked on a schedule but need an app now

The block screen always offers five minutes for emergencies.

Which home screen look should I use?

Default. Clear and Tinted take the colour out of widgets. Aglo's wallpapers (Settings, Wallpaper & look) are made to sit behind the sky.

How do I stop using Aglo?

Remove apps from Guarded apps, or delete Aglo. Deleting it removes every block straight away.

Cancel Aglo Plus

iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Aglo. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
